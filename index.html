Quick rules

How to play Ashen Duel

  1. On your turn, mana refills and grows by one. Draw one card.
  2. Click a card in your hand to summon a creature or cast a spell. Traps are set face down and trigger when attacked.
  3. Click one of your creatures, then an enemy creature to attack it. If their field is empty, attack their life directly.
  4. Press End Turn to let your opponent act. Reduce their life to zero to win.
  5. Earn souls from victories and buy signature cards or healing at the Soul Merchant.
